The energy transition of apartment buildings is a major challenge in Belgium. Between ever stricter EPC (PEB) standards and soaring fossil fuel prices, co-owned buildings are looking for sustainable alternatives. At **WellDoneDrill**, we install vertical drilling systems capable of heating and cooling entire buildings. Here is why geothermal is the most relevant solution for multi-unit housing.

### 1. No more noise and visual clutter

This is the number one problem with air-to-water heat pumps in dense urban areas: noise. Installing dozens of outdoor units on balconies or the roof creates unbearable noise for the neighbours and spoils the building's façade.

- **Complete discretion:** With geothermal, everything happens underground and in the basement. There is no outdoor fan, no vibration and nothing visible from the street.
- **Respecting planning rules:** In Brussels or historic town centres in Wallonia, planning regulations often ban air-conditioning units on façades. Geothermal elegantly avoids this constraint.

### 2. Geocooling: summer comfort without higher service charges

Global warming makes apartments (especially top-floor ones) stifling in summer. Installing conventional air conditioning in every unit sends shared electricity costs through the roof.

- **Passive cooling:** The network of probes at 150 metres circulates 12 °C water through the apartments' underfloor heating.
- **Economies of scale:** This cooling is almost free (only the circulator uses a little energy). It is a strong selling point for landlords and unrivalled comfort for residents.

### 3. Sharing the borehole field to cut costs

A big advantage of co-ownership is **pooling the investment**. Instead of each owner installing their own system, a shared geothermal borehole field is created.

- **Optimised space:** Several 150-metre boreholes (a borehole field) are drilled under the car park or the shared garden of the residence.
- **Tailored output:** The geothermal plant in the basement distributes heat according to the actual needs of each apartment, optimising the building's overall efficiency.

### 4. An immediate boost to property value

A building heated by geothermal energy benefits from an **outstanding EPC rating**.

- **On resale:** An apartment rated A or B sells faster and for more than one heated by gas or oil.
- **Energy independence:** The co-ownership is freed from fuel deliveries and geopolitical swings in gas prices. The "fuel" (the Earth's heat) belongs to the co-ownership.

### 5. An installation that (literally) lasts a lifetime

While a communal boiler must be replaced every 15 years, the borehole field built by **WellDoneDrill** is guaranteed for more than 50 years. It is a structural investment in the building. Only the heat pump in the plant room will need servicing or replacement in the long run; the ground loop infrastructure stays in the ground for generations.

### Conclusion

For a co-owned building, geothermal is not an expense but a strategy to protect the building's value. It offers premium comfort (heating and cooling), ends neighbour disputes over noise and cuts energy bills by a factor of four.
